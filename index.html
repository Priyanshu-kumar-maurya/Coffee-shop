Our master roasters meticulously slow-roast every batch to unlock complex notes of dark cocoa, roasted hazelnuts, and wild summer berries.

The Four Pillars of Our Coffee Craft

01

High Altitude Harvesting

Hand-harvested red ripe cherries grown above 1,800m altitude for dense, nutrient-rich flavor profiles.

02

Small-Batch Drum Roasting

Slow roasted in cast-iron Giesen roasters under precise thermal curves to highlight bean terroir.

03

Filtered Mineral Water

Triple reverse-osmosis water remineralized with calcium and magnesium for optimal flavor extraction.

04

Master Barista Precision

Precise grams, temperature, and extraction seconds dialed in every morning before first pour.
